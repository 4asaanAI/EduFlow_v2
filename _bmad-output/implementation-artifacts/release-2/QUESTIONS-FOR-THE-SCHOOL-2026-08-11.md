## Please read this first

We have loaded the school's fee information onto the new platform from the school's own
records: the printed 2026-27 fee sheet, the fee reports, the student list and the payment

printout of this page.

## 4. Brothers and sisters: 377 families confirmed, and some loose ends

The office has been writing sibling links by hand for years, in the remark on a payment,
usually as `SIB NO - 221858`. **We have used only those.** We have not guessed at families
by matching surnames or phone numbers, because two unrelated children can share either.

From the school's own remarks we have confirmed **377 families covering 826 children**,
and we have tagged each child's record with the admission numbers of their brothers and
sisters. That is the tag Sonu asked for, and it now shows on the child's record and on the
fee screen.

**Who keeps the concession has been copied from what the office actually did**, not worked
out by us. 445 children were given the sibling concession in the ledger and keep it. The
other 381 in those families are the ones the office charged in full, which matches the
school's rule that the youngest child pays full.

Three loose ends need a person.

### 4a. 58 children were given a sibling concession, but no sibling was ever named

These children received the sibling discount in the ledger, and no remark anywhere says
who their brother or sister is.

**We have NOT given them the concession on the new platform**, because we were asked to
use only the families the school has itself stated, and for these there is no statement to
use.

**What we need:** for each of these children, the admission number of their brother or
sister in the school. Once we have that, the concession goes back on automatically.

The full list of 58 admission numbers is in the file
`aaryans_database/_sibling_links.json` under `discounted_with_no_stated_family`, and we can
print it in any form that is easier to work with.

### 4b. 33 families where the fee does not follow the school's own rule

The rule is that exactly one child in a family, the youngest, pays the full fee. In 33 of
the 377 families that is not what the records show.

- **In 17 families, every child was given the concession** and nobody paid full. For
  example, admission numbers 242311, 242312 and 262993 are recorded as one family, and all
  three were discounted.
- **In 16 families, two or more children paid full.** One of these is a group of eight
  children, which is more likely to be two different families that got joined together by
  a remark than one family of eight.

**What we need:** are these deliberate, or are they mistakes in the old system? We have
left them exactly as the office recorded them rather than correcting them ourselves, since
either way it is somebody's money.

### 4c. 27 remarks mention a sibling and we could not read a number out of them

For example: `REG NO 3453 DIS AGAINST OTHER TWO STUDENT SIB DIS` names no admission number
at all. We have reported these rather than making them fit.

## 5. The school bus: two questions about the rates

We have loaded **48 routes, 185 stops and 1,376 children** as bus users, from the school's
own student list. Before this, the platform said not one child used the bus, which was
plainly wrong.

### 5a. Are 1,900 and 620 a month real rates?

The school's records show 35 different monthly bus rates. Most sit between 650 and 1,520,
which is what we expected. Two sit outside that: **five payments at 1,900 a month**, and
**one child at 620 a month**.

**What we need:** are these real rates for long or short distances, or are they old typing
errors?

### 5b. Four payments at rates that match no child's record

Two rates, **1,170 and 1,680 a month**, appear in the payment ledger but match nothing in
the student list. Only four payments are involved, so this is small.

The likely explanation is dull: the student list was exported on 6 August and the ledger
runs to 11 August, so a rate may have been corrected in between. **We would rather ask
than assume.**

### 5c. Three children have a bus stop and no route number

Their stops are recorded as `SINORA`, `JAMA PUR` and `MOHANPUR` with no route against
them. They are admission numbers **201153, 242305 and 242439**.

They have been given their stop and their fare. They have **no route**, because inventing
one is a guess about which bus a child actually rides, which matters on the day something
goes wrong.

**What we need:** which route each of these three children travels on.
