# EduFlow — your one page

**For Lalit Thomas, management, The Aaryans**

This is everything you need to start. One page, no jargon. Keep it by the computer.

---

## Signing in

Go to the school's EduFlow address and sign in with the login and password Abhimanyu
gives you in person. Nothing here is written down in a document, and nobody will ever
email or message you a password. If somebody does, it did not come from us.

If you are locked out, ask Aman or Adesh. They are the only two who can reset a password.

---

## What the platform is for you

You keep the school's day-to-day record straight, and most of the platform is yours:

- **Students and staff.** Add them, edit them, correct them, keep the details current.
- **Admissions.** Enquiries, follow-ups, the funnel, and moving a child through it.
- **Talking to families.** Circulars, announcements and direct messages to parents.
- **Academics.** Classes and sections, the timetable, exams, quizzes, houses and
  activities, and marking student attendance.
- **People.** Staff attendance, staff leave, performance records.
- **The campus.** Library, rooms, assets, stock, facility requests and maintenance.
- **Reports and the odds and ends.** Scheduled reports, incidents and visitors, the query
  desk, forms and tech issues.

---

## What you never see, and it is not an accident

**You do not see any rupee figure, anywhere.** Not fees collected, not what a family owes,
not expenses, not anybody's salary. That is Aman's decision and it is the simplest line
the school could draw.

**What you do get instead**: open any child's record and it tells you whether their fees
are **Paid**, **Unpaid** or **Overdue**. No amount, just the flag. That is what you need to
chase a family, and it is enough.

**One exception.** You can look up the school's published fee rate card, meaning what a
class is charged per year. That is on the school's own fee sheet and any parent can ask
for it. It says nothing about any particular family.

## Certificates and ID cards

You can make a certificate or a batch of ID cards, and **Aman or Adesh has to approve it
before it prints**. Either one of them is enough.

So the order is: you fill it in, you press the button, it says it has gone for approval,
and once one of them approves it the Download button works. If you press Download before
that, the platform tells you it is still waiting. That is not a fault.

For ID cards you tick the children you need and press "Ask for approval" once for the
whole batch, not once per child. When it comes back approved, it appears in a short list
at the top of the screen with a Download button.

Sports and participation certificates are the exception. They are awards, so they print
straight away.

## Flo, the assistant

Flo is the chat box. You can type at it the way you would ask a colleague:

- "Add a new student to class 6-B."
- "Which children in class 9 were absent this week?"
- "Send a circular to all parents about Monday's holiday."
- "Show me the enquiries from last week that nobody has followed up."

**Anything that changes a record will ask you to confirm first.** Read the confirmation
before you press yes: it tells you what is about to happen and, for anything that reaches
many families at once, how many.

If Flo says it cannot do something, it will tell you who can. If it ever refuses something
you believe is your job, that is worth reporting rather than working around.

---

## If you make a mistake

**You can put back your own change, on the same day.** Find it under your own recent
changes and press undo. It only works on your own change, only today, and not on money,
on whether a child is on the roll, or on anybody's login. If it will not undo, it tells
you why in a sentence, and Adesh can fix the rest.

**You cannot delete a child or a colleague, on purpose.** You type all day and a slip of
the hand should not take a child off the school's roll. If somebody has left, mark them as
left. If a record is genuinely wrong and needs removing, that is Adesh's or Aman's.

---

## Three things worth knowing on day one

1. **You are working on the school's real data.** There is no practice copy. What you type
   is what the school has, for 1,842 children.
2. **Every change is recorded**, with your name and the time. That is not surveillance; it
   is how a mistake gets found and put right instead of argued about.
3. **If something looks wrong, say so early.** Nobody will mind you asking.
