# What each staff role should be able to do — draft for Aman

**For:** Aman Litt, owner, The Aaryans
**From:** Layaa AI, 2026-08-10
**Why:** so that when the receptionist, the transport head, the IT person, the maintenance
person and the office helpers get their logins, each one opens onto their own job and
nothing else. Nobody is switched on until you say yes to what is below.

**How to read this.** Each role has three parts: what they can do, what they deliberately
cannot, and a question or two for you. Please correct anything that does not match how The
Aaryans actually runs. Where we were unsure we have guessed narrow, on the principle that
it is easier to give somebody one more screen than to take back something they have seen.

**Nothing here changes who is already on the platform.** You, Adesh, Sonu and Lalit are
covered by a separate plan.

---

## Where they fit

| Release | Who gets a login |
|---|---|

## 1. Transport head — Chaman Singh

**Can do**

- See and change bus routes, stops, vehicles, and which driver and conductor is on which
  bus.
- See which children are on which route, and their pick-up and drop points.
- Use the route planning tool that works out better stop groupings.
- See the phone numbers and addresses of the families on his routes, because he needs to
  call a parent when a bus is late.
- See his drivers' and conductors' details, as their direct supervisor.
- Report a problem with a bus or with school property.

**Cannot do**

- **See any money at all**, including what a family pays for transport. Transport fees are
  Sonu's, not his. He decides who rides which bus; Sonu decides what that costs.
- See children's academic records, attendance, exam results or fee status.
- See staff outside his own transport team.
- Add, remove or edit a child's record beyond their transport details.

**Questions for you**

1. Should Chaman be able to move a child from one route to another on his own, or should
   that need Adesh's or Lalit's agreement first?
2. Does he handle vehicle servicing and repair bills himself? If he does, he will need the
   maintenance schedule too, and we would want to agree how that stays separate from
   seeing costs.

## 2. Receptionist — front desk

**Can do**

- Look up any child: name, class, section, guardian names and phone numbers, so she can
  answer the phone and the gate.
- Log visitors in and out, and record who they came to see.
- Take an admission enquiry from a walk-in or a phone call and record it, then log the
  follow-up calls.
- Send a message to a parent, for example that a child is unwell or that pick-up is
  delayed.
- See today's attendance, to answer "is my child in school today".
- Record an incident at the gate or in reception.
- Report a problem with school property.

**Cannot do**

- **See any fee amount or any salary.** Not even whether a family has paid. A parent asking
  about fees at the front desk gets directed to Sonu.
- Issue a Transfer Certificate or any other school document.
- Add, edit or remove a child's record.
- See exam results, marks or teacher records.

**Questions for you**

1. Today the platform offers the receptionist the Student Transfer screen, which is how a
   Transfer Certificate is issued. **We think that is wrong and want to remove it.** Do you
   agree that issuing a TC is not a front-desk decision?
2. She is also offered Commercial Operations, which covers the school's legal entities and
   the campus shop tills. **We think that is wrong too and want to remove it.** Unless the
   reception desk actually runs the shop counter, in which case tell us and we will give
   her the till and nothing else.
3. When a parent rings and asks whether fees are cleared, what should she say? At the
   moment our answer is "please speak to Sonu". If you would rather she could see a simple
   paid or not-paid mark, with no amounts, we can do that, the same as Lalit has.

## 3. IT and technical support

**Can do**

- See and work the queue of technical problems staff report: projector not working, printer
  down, network trouble.
- See the register of computers, projectors and other equipment, and who currently has
  each item.
- Report a problem with school property.
- See the staff list as names, departments and phone numbers only, so he knows whose
  machine he is fixing.

**Cannot do**

- **See any child's personal details at all.** Not names, not addresses, not guardians.
  Fixing a computer does not require the children's records, and the school holds data on
  1,876 children who are minors.
- See any fee or salary figure.
- See attendance, marks or exam results.
- Change anybody's password or create any login. Password resets stay with you and Adesh.

**Questions for you**

1. Is there a single IT person at The Aaryans, or is this handled by an outside contractor
   who visits? If it is an outsider, we would recommend an even narrower profile and an
   account you can switch off between visits.
2. Should he be able to close a technical ticket himself, or should the person who raised
   it confirm it is fixed?

---

## 4. Maintenance and upkeep

**Can do**

- See and work the queue of repair and maintenance requests from around the campus.
- See and update the preventive maintenance calendar: which building work is due when.
- See the list of contractors and service providers, and their phone numbers.
- Report a new problem.
- See the register of school equipment and tools.

**Cannot do**

- **See any money.** No contractor bills, no quoted amounts, no expenses. He knows who to
  call; Sonu knows what it costs.
- See any child's or teacher's personal records.
- See fees, salaries, attendance or academics.

**Questions for you**

1. Today the platform lets the maintenance person **edit** the contractor and vendor list.
   Sonu is now the owner of vendor records, because vendor payments are his. **We propose
   the maintenance person can see contractors and their numbers, but only Sonu, you and
   Adesh can add, change or remove one.** Does that match how it works at the school?
2. Who approves a repair before it happens? If maintenance can raise it but somebody must
   agree before a contractor is called, we will build that step in rather than leaving it
   to a phone call.

## 5. Office helpers and support staff

This is the one we are least sure about, and it is also the one that is most wrong today.

**What is wrong now.** The platform has no list for this role at all, so anyone given it is
currently offered nearly the whole admin menu. Most of the buttons do not work when
pressed, because the server refuses them, but the menu should never have offered them. We
are fixing it either way.

**What we propose they can do**

- See their own record: their attendance, their leave, their payslip.
- Apply for leave.
- Report a problem with school property.
- Read school announcements and circulars.

**Cannot do**

- Everything else. No children's records, no other staff, no money, no academics.

**Questions for you**

1. Who at The Aaryans would hold this profile? Peons, drivers, cleaning and canteen staff,
   or something wider?
2. Do these colleagues need a login at all in the first year, or would you rather they stay
   off the platform until the teachers are on? There is no harm in defining the profile now
   and giving nobody the login.
3. Should they be able to message the office through the platform's staff chat, or is that
   only for people with a desk?

---

## Two things that apply to all five

**Nobody below Adesh can change a password or create a login.** Those stay with you and
Adesh. If a colleague is locked out, they come to one of you.

**Nobody below Sonu sees a single rupee.** That is the simplest line we can draw and the
easiest one to keep. If any of these five genuinely needs to see an amount to do their job,
tell us which one and why, and we will open exactly that and nothing more.
