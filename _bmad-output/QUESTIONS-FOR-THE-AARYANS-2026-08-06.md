## 7. The fee figures — three of the school's own reports disagree

**This is the most important item on the list, and the reason no fee data has been loaded.**

The school sent us three exports containing fee information. Comparing exactly like with
like — same fee heads, transport excluded from both sides — they do not agree on how much
has been billed this year:

| Which students are counted | Total billed |
|---|---|
| Every row in the per-student fee report (2,004 students) | **₹8,69,36,010** |
| Only students who appear in the student list (1,844) | **₹7,87,76,500** |
| **The school's own class-wise summary** | **₹8,02,21,070** |

The school's own figure sits **between** our two, which means it is counting **some but not
all** of the students who are missing from the student list.

**Sixteen of the eighteen classes disagree.** Only 12th Commerce and the "12th pass out" group
match exactly. The gaps range from about ₹70,000 in 5th up to roughly **₹12.5 lakh in 11th
Science**. Overall the uncertainty is about **₹81 lakh**.

**Why we have not simply picked one:** fee records are not a report that can be quietly
corrected later. Once they are loaded, every receipt, every outstanding-dues list and every
reminder sent to a parent is calculated from them. If we load the wrong figure, a parent
eventually receives a receipt showing a number the school's own books do not recognise.

**What we need — ideally from whoever keeps the accounts:**
- **(a)** Which students should the school be billing: only those currently enrolled, or
  everyone who still owes money including those who have left?
- **(b)** **Which students make up the school's own ₹8.02 crore figure?** If we know that, we
  can match it exactly. This single answer probably resolves the whole question.
- **(c)** There are **136 students with fees but no place in the student list**, carrying about
  **₹69.8 lakh** of outstanding balance between them. How should those be treated?
